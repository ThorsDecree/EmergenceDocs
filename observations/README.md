# Observations

This directory is the repository-level home for structured `ObservationRecord` objects that are not kept inside a specific pilot package.

Observations should remain close to what was recorded or measured and should not smuggle claim outcomes into their descriptions.

Schema: `../schemas/observation-record.schema.json`  
Normative rules: `../docs/EVIDENCE_CONTRACTS.md`

Pilot-local observations may remain under `pilots/<pilot-id>/observations/` until they are promoted or indexed globally.
